# May 2026 Meal Plan Final

## Day 1
**Category:** Bean & vegetarian
* **Primary:** Couscous with Chickpeas, Dates and Scallions
* **Fallback:** Baked Lentils With Cheese
* **Dessert:** mommom's chocolate bourbon-spiked banana bread
* **Notes:** Culinary Research: Chickpeas and dates are a classic Moroccan synergy. The sweetness of the dates balances the earthy chickpeas. Tip: For better texture, fluff the couscous with a fork and add a splash of high-quality olive oil before serving.

## Day 2
**Category:** chicken
* **Primary:** Flat Roast Chicken
* **Fallback:** Lemon Herb Chicken
* **Dessert:** Perfectly Pillowy Cinnamon Rolls
* **Notes:** Culinary Research: Spatchcocking (flattening) the chicken ensures even cooking and crispier skin. Leftover roast chicken is ideal for sandwiches or salads later in the week. Synergy: Use the lemon and herb aromatics from the chicken to inspire the flavor profile of tomorrow's salmon.

## Day 3
**Category:** fish
* **Primary:** broiled or grilled salmon teriyaki
* **Fallback:** Coconut-Curry Rice with Salmon and Cilantro
* **Dessert:** Snowball Cookies Recipe
* **Notes:** Culinary Research: Salmon teriyaki pairs well with the ginger and soy notes. Synergy: If you have leftover rice from Day 1, it can be repurposed here. Technique: Remove salmon from heat 2-3 minutes early to let carryover cooking finish it for a moister result.

## Day 4
**Category:** bean & beef
* **Primary:** Ground Beef and Black Bean Taco Soup
* **Fallback:** Pasta e Fagioli
* **Dessert:** The Best Pecan Pie
* **Notes:** Culinary Research: Using black beans in taco soup provides a creamy texture that complements the ground beef. Tip: Sauté the onions and beef together until deeply browned to build a rich base for the soup.

## Day 5
**Category:** vegetarian
* **Primary:** Blistered Cherry Tomato and Gooey Mozzarella Spaghetti
* **Fallback:** Lazy Veggie Lasagna
* **Dessert:** Blueberry Crumble with Oats and Tahini
* **Notes:** Culinary Research: Blistering cherry tomatoes in olive oil creates a natural, sweet sauce that perfectly coats spaghetti. Technique: Salt the pasta water generously—it's the only chance to season the pasta itself.

## Day 6
**Category:** chicken
* **Primary:** Honey-Mustard Chicken Bake
* **Fallback:** Tuscan Chicken Pasta
* **Dessert:** 60-Second Chocolate Chip Mug Cookie
* **Notes:** Culinary Research: Honey-mustard glaze acts as both a flavor enhancer and a moisture barrier for the chicken. Synergy: Serve with a side of simple roasted vegetables to utilize the oven space.

## Day 7
**Category:** bean & vegetarian
* **Primary:** Spanish Chickpeas and Rice
* **Fallback:** black beans and rice

## Day 8
**Category:** pork

## Day 9
**Category:** fish
* **Primary:** Steamed Fish with Miso and Scallions
* **Fallback:** Garlic Butter Oven Baked Tilapia
* **Dessert:** golden granola

## Day 10
